About TutorPro Online English

Last updated: 4 August 2026

TutorPro Online English is an online English school for children and teenagers. Every lesson is one-to-one, so your child speaks for the whole class instead of waiting their turn in a group.

What we do

We teach Primary and Secondary learners in live, browser-based classes. Lessons are aligned with Cambridge and Oxford English curricula and adapted to each child's year level, school work and confidence.

How we teach

  • One-to-one only. No group classes, no shared attention.
  • Our own classroom. Lessons run inside our website — no Zoom, no downloads, no separate meeting links.
  • Structured courseware. Interactive slides, reading materials, pronunciation practice and speaking drills built into every class.
  • Visible progress. Parents get written teacher feedback after lessons, plus attendance, homework and progress tracking in their dashboard.

Our teachers

Teachers apply through a structured process that includes a recorded teaching interview and a review of their qualifications and experience. Parents can view teacher profiles, qualifications and introduction videos before booking.

Where we operate

TutorPro Online English is run as a registered sole proprietorship based in the Philippines and teaches families worldwide. Our teaching base is Manila time (UTC+8), and lesson times shown in your dashboard are automatically converted to your own local timezone.

Pricing you can see up front

Our rates are published on the website, not hidden behind a sales call. Classes start from $8 per 25-minute lesson, and every new family can take a free first class before paying anything. Full terms are on our Refund Policy page.

How teachers apply — and what we never do

We are aware that fake recruiters use the names of small online schools, including ours, to run job scams on messaging apps. Please read this before responding to any offer that claims to be from us.

We will never:

  • Charge a teacher any fee. No training fee, no registration fee, no deposit, no equipment payment. Not ever, for any reason.
  • Recruit through Telegram. We do not use Telegram at all.
  • Ask you to complete paid "tasks", top up a wallet, or move money on our behalf.
  • Ask for your bank password, OTP codes, or a copy of your card.

How real applications work

Every genuine teacher application goes through the Teacher portal on this website. It includes a structured application form and a recorded teaching interview. We review qualifications and teaching experience before approving anyone to teach.

If somebody contacted you about teaching for TutorPro Online English through Telegram, or asked you for money at any stage, it was not us. Please report it to the platform you were contacted on, and feel free to tell us so we can warn others.

How to verify us

  • Our only website is www.tutorpro.site. We have no other domain.
  • Our official Facebook page is facebook.com/tutorproenglish, where parents have left public recommendations.
  • Our pricing is published openly on our pricing page — we do not hide costs behind a private chat.
  • Our refund policy, terms and privacy policy are all published in full.
  • Payments from parents are processed by PayPal. We never ask a family to send money to a personal account.
