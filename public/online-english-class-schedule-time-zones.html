The honest position on North America

The Americas are the hardest fit. With a 12–16 hour difference, the workable window is usually an early-morning lesson before school, which suits some children and not others. If your child is not a morning person, a tutor closer to your own time zone may serve you better. We would rather say that now than have you find out after paying.

Lesson length

25 minutes suits younger children, 50 minutes suits older ones. You can switch between them as your child grows.

How often should lessons be?

For speaking, frequency matters more than duration. Two 25-minute lessons a week generally produce faster progress than one 50-minute lesson, because language is retained through repeated contact rather than long single sessions. Families aiming for noticeable improvement within a school term usually settle on two or three lessons a week.
