Kumu English: online English classes for Filipino kids

"Kumusta!" is how every TutorPro lesson begins. Parents searching for Kumu English are usually looking for exactly that: a friendly teacher who greets a Filipino child in a way that puts them at ease, then gets them speaking English from the first minute.

What "Kumu English" means to us

Kumu comes from kumusta — the Filipino greeting every child here hears at home. It is the sound of a warm start, and a warm start is exactly what a shy or hesitant young learner needs before they will speak a second language. So Kumu English, for us, is simply a name for the way we teach: greeting first, conversation first, confidence before correction.

Why Filipino kids speak more with a Filipino teacher

Our teachers are near-native Filipino English speakers who grew up learning English the same way your child is — as a second language, at school, with the same hesitations. They know what it feels like to be afraid of sounding wrong, which makes them unusually good at waiting for an answer instead of filling the silence.

Every lesson is one-to-one: one child, one teacher, no group to hide in and no group to be talked over by. That is the single biggest reason children speak more in our classes than in a group of six.

What a Kumu English lesson looks like

Twenty-five minutes, in the browser, nothing to install. The teacher greets your child, then the lesson follows a levelled Cambridge or Oxford series — real books, pictures, stories and repetition, paced to your child's attention span rather than to a page count.

When your child says "She don't like it", the teacher recasts it — "She doesn't like it? Why not?" — and the conversation continues. The correct form is supplied naturally and nobody is told they are wrong. After the lesson you get written feedback: what was practised, what went well, and what to revise at home.
