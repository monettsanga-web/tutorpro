Why families in Cebu City look for English tuition

Cebu households often speak Cebuano at home, so children can arrive at school with strong comprehension but little confidence producing English aloud. Cebu is also a major BPO and tourism hub, and many parents want their children fluent enough to work in those industries later.

Our lessons are built around building spoken confidence when English is not the language used at home. Children follow the K to 12 curriculum set by the Department of Education at school, and a tutor who understands that context is more useful than one teaching a generic syllabus.

What a lesson looks like

Your child speaks the whole lesson

In a class of forty a child might speak for three minutes. One-to-one, they speak for twenty-five. That is the entire difference in how quickly confidence grows.

Real coursebooks

Lessons follow Cambridge and Oxford books — Power Up, Global English, Family and Friends, Grammar Friends — not improvised worksheets.

Written feedback every time

After each class the teacher records what was practised, what went well and what to work on, with words to practise at home.

A teacher you chose

Questions from Cebu City parents

We speak Cebuano at home. Will that hold my child back?

No. Many of our Cebu students speak Cebuano at home and English only at school. Teachers expect that, start where the child actually is, and build spoken confidence from there rather than assuming prior fluency.

Can lessons help with BPO or tourism career preparation later?

For older teenagers, yes. Teachers can focus on clear pronunciation, telephone and customer-facing conversation, and confident everyday speech, which are the skills those industries actually test for.

Do you teach students outside Cebu City itself?

Yes. Lessons are online, so children anywhere in Cebu province or the wider Visayas can join with an ordinary internet connection.
