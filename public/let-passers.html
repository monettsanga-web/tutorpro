      LET results — questions visitors ask

      Where can I view LET results online?

      The official LET results are released by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) through its own channels. This page collects PDF files of the passers lists posted by TutorPro English PH, so you can open or download them free, without signing up.

      What is the difference between the Primary and Secondary LET?

      The Primary level is the licensure examination for elementary teachers; the Secondary level is for high school teachers. Each level has its own section above, with its own files.

      How often is this page updated?

      As soon as a new passers file is uploaded by TutorPro English PH it appears in its Primary or Secondary section — there is no refresh cycle to wait for.

      What if I cannot find the file I need?

      Message us through the contact page and we will send the file to you by email, usually within one business day. For background on the examination itself, read our guide: LET results — how to view the passers list online.
