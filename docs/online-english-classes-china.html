Our teachers

Near-native Filipino English teachers

Every TutorPro class is taught live by a Filipino teacher who speaks English at a near-native level. English is an official language of the Philippines - used in its schools, government and business - so our teachers have learnt, studied and worked in English all their lives, and teach it with a clear, neutral accent that children around the world find easy to follow.

  • Clear, near-native accent. Filipino English is known for being easy to understand - the reason families across Europe, the Middle East and Asia choose Filipino teachers for their children.
  • Warm, patient classrooms. Filipino teaching culture prizes patience and encouragement, which is exactly what a shy or beginner child needs before they will speak.
  • Checked before they meet your child. Every teacher passes a recorded teaching interview and a credential check before their first class.

English at school in China

English is part of the curriculum from primary school and the pressure rises through 初中. Lessons can support the school book and the speaking practice that large classes make difficult, or work separately on reading, phonics and conversation.

How this differs from what you have locally

Classes run in our own browser classroom with a low-bandwidth mode. If cross-border video is unstable, ask the administrator for a VooV / 腾讯会议 link instead — the lesson is the same either way.

Who we are usually asked to help in China

  • A primary pupil who needs phonics and reading before the pressure of 初中
  • A child whose school English is strong on paper and thin in conversation
  • A family who wants an English teacher without a cross-border video that freezes

Questions from Chinese parents

Will the video connection work from China?

The classroom has a low-bandwidth mode built in, and a VooV / Tencent Meeting link is available as a backup. Families who have trouble with one usually find the other works.

Can we pay in RMB?

Yes. QR payment is RMB25 per 25-minute lesson plus an RMB5 processing fee per session. Send the receipt and the credits are added after the administrator verifies it.

Can we communicate in Chinese?

Yes. The support chat on the site answers in Chinese, and the administrator replies in Chinese. The lessons themselves are taught in English.

Is there a Chinese version of this website?

Yes — the Chinese pages are at /cn/, with the same information in Simplified Chinese.

What time are lessons available?

China and the Philippines share UTC+8, so there is no time difference and any after-school slot works.
