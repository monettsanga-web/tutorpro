Lesson times from Germany

Germany runs on CET (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer). Manila is 6-7 hours ahead, so a 16:00 lesson in Berlin is 22:00 or 23:00 for the teacher - precisely the window our night-shift teachers open, which is why European after-school slots exist at all.

Slots appear in your own local time when you book. How scheduling works across time zones

English at school in Germany

English becomes a compulsory subject in most German states from class 3 or 5, and Gymnasium expectations rise quickly. Lessons can follow the school book, or build the speaking confidence German classrooms rarely have time to practise.

How this differs from what you have locally

Nachhilfe usually means a small group after an already long school day, with travel on top. These lessons are one-to-one, from home, and the whole 25 or 50 minutes belongs to your child.

Who we are usually asked to help in Germany

  • A Grundschule child meeting English for the first time in class 3
  • A Gymnasium pupil whose grades slip as the texts get harder
  • A family replacing costly Nachhilfe hours with focused one-to-one time

Lessons cover speaking, listening, reading, pronunciation, grammar and vocabulary, weighted towards whichever of those your child actually needs. How the classes work
