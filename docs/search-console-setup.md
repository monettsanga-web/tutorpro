# Getting your new pages found — the 15 minutes only you can do

Your site now has **53 pages**. Google will find them eventually on its own.
Telling it directly turns "eventually" into days.

Everything below needs your login, so I cannot do any of it for you. None of

## 1. Submit the sitemap to Google (2 minutes)

You are already verified with Google, so this is quick.

1. Go to **https://search.google.com/search-console**
2. Pick **www.tutorpro.site** from the property dropdown
3. Click **Sitemaps** in the left menu
4. Type `sitemap.xml` into the box and press **Submit**

If it already shows a sitemap, click it and press **Resubmit** — that nudges
Google to re-read all 53 URLs.

### Then ask Google to look at the important new pages

Still in Search Console, paste each of these into the **search bar at the very
top**, wait for it to load, then click **Request indexing**:

```
https://www.tutorpro.site/
https://www.tutorpro.site/free-trial.html
https://www.tutorpro.site/cambridge-english.html
https://www.tutorpro.site/oxford-english.html
https://www.tutorpro.site/blog/
```

Five is enough. There is a daily limit, and the rest will be found through
your sitemap and internal links anyway.

**Do the homepage first.** Its title and heading changed, and you want Google
to see the new version quickly.

## 2. Claim Bing (10 minutes) — currently missing entirely

Bing is not just Bing. It powers **Yahoo, DuckDuckGo and Microsoft Copilot**.
Right now none of them can be managed, and you cannot see whether they are
indexing you at all.

**The shortcut:** Bing can import everything from Google, so you do not repeat
the work.

1. Go to **https://www.bing.com/webmasters**
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account (any personal one is fine)
3. Choose **Import from Google Search Console**
4. Allow access when Google asks
5. Select **www.tutorpro.site** and import

That carries over your verification and your sitemap in one step.

If the import fails, use **Add site manually** instead. Bing will offer three
verification methods — choose the **meta tag** option, paste the tag it gives
you into a message to me, and I will add it to the site. It looks like this:

```html
<meta name="msvalidate.01" content="SOME-LONG-CODE-HERE" />
```

**Do not invent or guess this code** — it only works if it comes from Bing.

## The thing that actually limits you now

Your on-page SEO is in good shape: 53 pages, clean structured data, no
duplicate titles, no orphan pages, correct prices everywhere, breadcrumbs,
a working 404.

**No other website links to yours.** That is now the ceiling.

Search engines treat links as votes. A site with excellent pages and zero
links behaves like a shop on a street with no road to it. The fix is not more
pages — it is a handful of genuine mentions:

- Your own **Facebook page**, linking to a guide rather than the homepage
- **Parent groups** you are already in — sharing a useful article, not an advert
- A **local business directory** or two in the Philippines
- Any teacher or parent who would happily mention you if asked

Five real links would do more for your rankings than another fifty pages.

**The new articles are the first pages you have that anyone would plausibly
link to** — nobody links to a pricing page, but a genuinely useful guide on
helping a child speak English does get shared.

I can draft those posts for you whenever you want. Publishing them is the part
only you can do.
