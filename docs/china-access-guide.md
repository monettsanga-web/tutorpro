# Making TutorPro accessible in mainland China

Audit and action plan, based on scanning the live site for services blocked by the
Great Firewall.

---

## The honest summary

There is **no free or instant fix**. Real access in mainland China requires either an
**ICP licence** (a Chinese government filing) or a **China-adjacent CDN**. Everything else
is partial mitigation.

The good news: your site has fewer blocking dependencies than most, and the critical path
is already clean.

---

## What I found

### ✅ Not a problem — critical path is clear

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Google Fonts | **Not used.** Fonts are system/self-hosted. This is the single most common cause of Chinese pages hanging for 20+ seconds. |
| Google Analytics / Tag Manager | Not present |
| Facebook SDK | Links only, no SDK script. Degrades to a dead link, does not block rendering. |
| Vercel hosting | Not blocked outright, but slow — no mainland CDN nodes |
| Supabase (auth, database, storage) | Reachable, but latency is high without a China node |

### 🔴 Fixed today

**Referral QR codes** were generated by `api.qrserver.com`, which is unreliable in China.
Chinese parents saw a broken image where the QR should be — and QR sharing on WeChat is the
main way Chinese families share links, so this was the worst possible feature to break there.

QR codes are now **generated locally in the browser** using the `qrcode` package. No external
request, works offline, and the code never changes for the same referral link.

### 🟡 Degrades gracefully — acceptable

| Service | Impact in China | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube embeds (teacher intro videos) | Video will not play | Host key videos on **Bilibili or Tencent Video** for the China page, or upload directly to Supabase Storage |
| Trustpilot widget | Will not load | Renders nothing — no broken box. Fine. |
| Microsoft Office viewer (PPT in classroom) | Slow or fails | Convert PPT to PDF before class; the PDF viewer is local (`pdfjs`) and works |
| MyMemory translation API | Chat translation may fail | Falls back to original text, no crash |

## Your options, cheapest first

### Option 1 — Tencent EdgeOne Pages *(already prepared)*

Your repo already contains `edgeone.json` and `CHINA_DEPLOYMENT.md`. I have corrected the
one-click deploy link, which previously pointed at a stale branch.

- **Global (excluding mainland China) nodes:** no ICP licence needed. Noticeably faster than
  Vercel from China, but still crosses the border.
- **Mainland China nodes:** requires a custom domain, identity verification and an **ICP
  filing**. This is the only path to genuinely fast, reliable access.

Deploy link and settings are in `CHINA_DEPLOYMENT.md`. Build command is
`npm run build:edgeone`, output `dist`.

### Option 2 — ICP filing (the real solution)

An **ICP备案** is a filing with China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.
Without it, no domain can legally be served from inside mainland China.

Practical reality:
- Normally requires a **Chinese business entity** or a Chinese partner/agent
- Takes roughly **2–6 weeks**
- Must be paired with a Chinese host (Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud)
- Your DTI registration does **not** substitute for it

Worth doing only if China becomes a core market rather than a handful of families.

### Option 3 — WeChat-first, website-second *(recommended starting point)*

For a small number of Chinese families, do not fight the firewall. Meet them where they are.

1. **WeChat Official Account** — this is how Chinese parents actually discover and follow a
   school. Content inside WeChat loads instantly with no firewall issue.
2. **Keep `/cn/` as the landing page.** It already exists, is in Chinese, and is lightweight.
3. **Share lesson materials through WeChat** rather than relying on in-site downloads.
4. **VooV Meeting (腾讯会议) as the classroom fallback.** Your platform already supports a
   VooV link per teacher. Browser WebRTC is unreliable in China; VooV is the local standard
   and works.

## Practical advice for Chinese families today

Until an ICP filing exists, tell Chinese parents:

- **Use Chrome or Edge**, not the in-app WeChat browser — WeChat's browser blocks several
  WebRTC features the classroom needs.
- **Expect the first load to be slow.** Once loaded, the app is client-side and stays responsive.
- **If the classroom will not connect, use the VooV link** the teacher provides.
- **Convert PowerPoint to PDF** before lessons — PDFs render locally and reliably.

## Recommended sequence

1. ✅ **Done —** local QR generation, so referral sharing works in China
2. **Deploy to EdgeOne Pages** (global nodes, no ICP) and test from a real China connection —
   free, takes about 30 minutes
3. **Create a WeChat Official Account** — the highest-value China channel by far
4. **Only pursue an ICP filing** once you have enough Chinese families to justify the effort
   and a Chinese entity or agent to file it

## What I could not verify

I cannot test from inside mainland China from this environment. Before committing to any
option, test the real experience:

- Ask an existing Chinese family to load `https://www.tutorpro.site/cn/` and report timing
- Or use a China-based testing service such as `chinafirewalltest.com` or
  `webkaka.com` speed test with a mainland node

Real user feedback beats any prediction about firewall behaviour.
