## First: where in the world can you actually teach?

You teach from Manila. A market only works if their after-school hours (roughly
15:00–20:00 local) land inside your working day. Here is the real overlap:

| Verdict | Markets | Their 15:00–21:00 = your Manila time |
|---|---|---|
| **Best** | Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Taiwan | 15:00–21:00 — identical, no gap at all |
| **Best** | Japan, South Korea | 14:00–20:00 — one hour ahead, very comfortable |
| **Best** | Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam | 16:00–22:00 |
| **Best** | Australia, New Zealand | 11:00–19:00 — the easiest of all |
| **Best** | India | 17:00–23:00 |
| **Good (morning shift)** | US West Coast, Mexico | Their evening = your 07:00–12:00 |
| **Good (late shift)** | UAE, Saudi, Turkey | Their afternoon = your 19:00–23:00 |
| **Hard** | UK, Germany, Poland, Spain | Only 21:00–23:00 Manila — one or two slots |
| **Hard** | US East Coast, Brazil | Only 07:00–09:00 Manila |

**What this means practically:** aim at **Asia-Pacific first**. Same time zone, huge
demand for English, and parents who already budget for tutoring. Europe and the US
East Coast are not impossible, but you can only serve a couple of slots a day, so do
not spend your posting effort there yet.

**Also note:** mainland China restricts for-profit English tutoring for grades 1–9 and
bars foreign-based teachers for minors. Target the **overseas Chinese diaspora**
(Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Australia, Canada) rather than mainland China.

## 9. Group rules — how not to get banned

Most parenting groups ban outright advertising, and being removed costs you the whole
group permanently.

- **Read the pinned rules first.** Every time.
- **Comment helpfully for a week before you ever post a link.** Answer parents' questions
  about English learning with genuinely useful answers and no pitch.
- **Answer the question, then mention what you do.** "Try reading the same short book three
  nights in a row — repetition builds confidence faster than new material. I teach English
  one-to-one if you ever want a hand, but honestly that trick alone helps a lot."
- **One post per group per month, maximum.**
- **If a group bans promotion, do not post.** Be useful, let people check your profile.

## 10. A realistic expectation

Rough shape, not a promise:

```
100 people see a post
 → ~10 click
 →  ~3 register
 →  ~2 attend the free class
 →  ~1 becomes a paying family
```

So roughly **one paying family per 100 views**, and around **ten good posts a month** to
add one family. That sounds slow — but a monthly-plan family is about $120 a month, they
stay for months, and referrals compound.

**The follow-up emails matter more than the posts.** A parent who takes a free class and
hears nothing afterwards usually just drifts away. Check **Admin → Follow-ups** every couple
of days and send what is queued — that is where most of your revenue is won.
