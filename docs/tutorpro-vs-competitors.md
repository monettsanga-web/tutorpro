```

```

**14 KB of HTML, 0.26s.** Allright ships 437 KB and takes 1.31s — **31x heavier and 5x

respectively) with no dedicated policy page found. Cambly had none at common paths.

Given the "is this a scam?" concern AI assistants raised about you, this is now a genuine
strength — not a gap.

### 4. Price

**$8/class.** Novakid's *cheapest* tier is $9, Premium runs $17–20. You undercut the market
leader and it is invisible to search engines.

## Where you are losing — and why

### 🔴 The content gap is the whole problem

```

Novakid   █████████████████████████████████        3,392
Cambly    █████████                                  899
TutorPro  ████                                       419
```

Your 419 words are **good** — I read them. The hero, the one-to-one differentiator, the
Cambridge/Oxford alignment, the Novakid/Preply comparison paragraph, the FAQ. The writing is
clear and honest.

**There is simply not enough of it.** Google cannot rank a page for keywords it never
mentions, and 419 words covers very few keywords.

### 🔴 13 internal links vs Allright's 183

Internal links tell Google which pages matter and spread authority through the site. Allright
has **14x more**. You have almost nothing to link *to* — which is really a symptom of having
almost no pages.

### 🔴 Zero hreflang, despite being the most international of the four

You serve the Philippines, have a dedicated `/cn/` Chinese page, and are about to advertise
in Poland. **You have 0 hreflang tags.** Novakid has 47. Google currently has no signal about
which country should see which version of your site.

### 🔴 No age-segmented pages

All three competitors segment by age. Allright has ten: `/4-6-years`, `/7-9-years`,
`/10-12-years` and so on. A parent searching *"English lessons for 7 year old"* lands on a
page written for exactly that child. You have nothing for that query.

### 🔴 No visible testimonials

All three show named parents or students with locations. You show none, despite having a
rating system already built into the platform.

## The honest diagnosis

**Your product is not behind. Your website is.**

You are cheaper than Novakid, genuinely one-to-one where they upsell group classes, faster
than all three, better structured than two of them, and better documented on trust and policy
than any of them.

None of that is discoverable, because the homepage says 419 words and the site has 8 pages
while Allright has over 1,500.

This is a **content volume problem**, not a quality, product, or technical problem. That is
the good news — content is the one thing entirely within your control.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't chase `online english for kids`.** Novakid and Allright have years of backlinks.
  You will lose, and burn months doing it.
- **Don't mass-generate 286 city pages** like Allright. At your current authority that reads
  as doorway-page spam and risks a penalty. Ten Polish cities with genuinely distinct content
  is the safe version.
- **Don't add AggregateRating to Organization schema.** Already covered — it is ineligible
  and risks a manual action.

Win on long-tail, age-specific, price-led and comparison keywords first. Build authority,
then move up.
