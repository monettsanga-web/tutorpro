All `.com` domains below were **checked live against the Verisign registry** and were
available at the time of writing. Availability changes fast — verify before deciding.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before renaming

You have **98 commits, 437 brand mentions across 52 files**, plus:

- `tutorpro.site` domain + Vercel deployment
- Facebook page `/tutorproenglish`
- Live PayPal merchant configuration
- Desktop app build identity
- Brand-new panda logo, favicon and PWA icons (just made)
- SEO pages and sitemap **just submitted to Google**

**The timing is the real issue.** You have just fixed crawlability and shipped five trust
pages. Google is about to crawl and start building history for `tutorpro.site`. Renaming
now throws that away and restarts the clock — right when it finally started ticking.

### The honest recommendation

**Keep TutorPro Online English.** Nothing in the Google AI response criticised your *name*. It
criticised missing legal pages (now fixed) and missing reviews (needs real customers).
A new name fixes neither and costs you your only SEO history.

**Rename only if** one of these is true:

- There is a trademark conflict with an existing "TutorPro" (there are several tutoring
  companies using this name — worth checking if any operate in your markets)
- You want `.com` instead of `.site` for credibility with parents
- You are pivoting the business

That middle reason is legitimate. `.site` does read as cheaper than `.com` to some
parents, and that may be quietly costing you trust.

## Option A — Keep the name, upgrade the domain (lowest risk)

Stay **TutorPro Online English**, but move to a stronger domain if one is free.
Only the display domain changes; brand, logo and Facebook page all survive.
Set a 301 redirect from `tutorpro.site` so existing SEO carries over.

This preserves everything and fixes the `.site` credibility issue in one step.

## If you do rename — the safe sequence

Renaming is a genuine project. Do it in this order to avoid breaking payments or losing users:

1. **Buy the domain first.** Do not announce anything until it is yours.
2. **Trademark check** in your key markets before spending on rebranding.
3. **Keep `tutorpro.site` and 301-redirect it** to the new domain permanently. This is
   how you carry SEO history across — do not let it expire.
4. **Update the code** — I can automate the 437 mentions across 52 files in one pass.
5. **New logo/favicon/PWA icons** — the panda can stay if the new name is animal-based.
6. **Update PayPal merchant details** so the name on parents' card statements matches the
   site. A mismatch here causes chargebacks and looks like fraud.
7. **Rename the Facebook page** (Facebook allows this, keeps followers and reviews).
8. **Email existing parents before switching** so they are not surprised by an unfamiliar
   name in their inbox and on their bank statement.
9. **Resubmit sitemap** in Google Search Console and use the Change of Address tool.

Step 6 and step 8 are the ones people forget, and they are the ones that cost money.
